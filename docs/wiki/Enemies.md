# Enemies

The gangs of Shin-Kagura come at you from everywhere: a beam of light marks
the spot, then they walk out of a metro tunnel, freight lift or roof hatch,
or fall in from 7 to 9 m up and land in a shower of sparks. They follow you
between levels, taking the ramps and leaping up ledges after a short crouch
([where they come from](The-Map#where-enemies-come-from)). Every attack is **telegraphed** with a windup, glowing eyes
and a sound, so you always get a chance to react.

## The horde

| Enemy | HP | Speed | Stagger | Armour | Points | XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Grunt** (Ashigaru) | 45 | 5.8 m/s | 1.8 s | none | 100 | 10 |
| **Gunner** (Teppo) | 80 | 4.8 m/s | 2.2 s | none | 150 | 18 |
| **Brute** (Kanabo) | 520 | 3.8 m/s | 2.8 s | katana does 35% | 600 | 60 |

Damage below is against your 100 HP on the first waves.

### Grunt (Ashigaru)

The foot soldiers. They rush straight at you and swing.

- **Swing:** a 0.5 s windup, then 11 damage in front of them (2.7 m).
- **Answer:** the katana cleaves through a pack of them. A grunt kill is your
  easiest cell.

### Gunner (Teppo)

Riflemen who strafe and keep 8 to 17 m away.

- **Shot:** with a clear line to you, a 0.72 s windup, then a glowing bolt
  (12 damage) that you can **deflect** back. **From wave 6** they fire a fan
  of 3 bolts.
- **Answer:** slash the bolts back for 8× damage and a cell, or close in and
  blast.
- First appear on wave 2.

### Brute (Kanabo)

Armoured heavies with a club, and the main reason to carry cells.

- **Swing:** a 0.82 s windup, then 26 damage (3.6 m).
- **Charge:** a roar and a 0.8 s windup, then a dash at 13.5 m/s for 30
  damage and a big knockback. They slam into walls. Every 6 to 8.5 s.
- **Answer:** the katana does only 35% until they stagger. **Shotgun them to
  stagger, then glory kill.** Dash sideways out of the charge. A kill is
  tagged **KANABO DOWN** and drops 4 health orbs.
- First appear on wave 4.

## They get tougher

Enemy health rises every wave: ×1.24 on wave 5, ×1.59 on wave 10 and faster
after that. Their damage rises half as fast. Waves also send more enemies at
once (see [Waves and Mutators](Waves-and-Mutators)). Some mutators change
enemies too, such as **KABUTO PLATE** (armoured grunts and gunners) and
**HANNYA'S WRATH** (wounded enemies enrage).

## Kill tags

Each kill flashes a tag with its points. The special ones:

| Tag | How |
| --- | --- |
| GLORY KILL | a [glory kill](Glory-Kills) (×2 points) |
| SAKAHOKO DROP | a [plunge](Glory-Kills#plunge) |
| HEADSHOT | a shotgun headshot kill (×1.5 points) |
| RETURN TO SENDER | killed by its own deflected shot (×1.5 points) |
| KANABO DOWN | a brute kill |
| CREMATED | burned to death (Kagutsuchi Flame, Homura Edge) |
| KAGE STEP | sliced by a dash (Kage Step card) |
| SUSANOO'S WAVE | cut by a blood blade (Susanoo's Wave card) |
