# Bosses

**Every third wave is a boss wave** (3, 6, 9 and on). An alarm sounds, **BOSS
INCOMING** flashes, and the boss's name card slides in with its own music.
Boss waves never have a mutator.

| | KUROKAGE | TETSUGUMO | SHUTEN |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Title | Blade of the Null Signal | Chrome Broodmother | the Overclocked Daemon |
| Role | Fast duelist | Zone controller | Heavyweight bruiser |
| Where it fights | Mid range, circling you | Far away (8-15 m) | Point blank |
| HP | 2600 | 4400 | 5200 |
| Katana damage until staggered | 55% | 35% | 50% |
| Speed | 5.2 m/s | 2.6 m/s | 4.4 m/s |
| Biggest single hit | 20 (dash) | 22 (stomp) | 32 (swing) |
| Jump over | nothing | the stomp ring | the leap slam ring |
| Dash out of | dash lines, blink strike | missile rings | the combo and the charge |
| Deflect back | shuriken fan | gatling bolts | fan volley (enraged) |
| Points / XP | 5000 / 320 | 7000 / 420 | 8000 / 500 |
| Felled line | SHADOW DELETED | MACHINE DECOMMISSIONED | DAEMON PURGED |

Damage numbers are against your 100 HP the first time you meet each boss.

## Rules every boss shares

- **Order:** the first cycle sends Kurokage and Tetsugumo on waves 3 and 6, in
  an order your [seed](Seeds) decides, and Shuten always on wave 9. After that
  all three come in a seeded shuffle.
- **They get tougher:** each time round a boss has more health, about ×1.18
  by wave 9 and ×1.72 by wave 12. Their damage rises half as fast.
- **Reinforcements:** 1 or 2 grunts drop in every 7 s (every 10 s against
  Tetsugumo) while fewer than 4 are alive.
- **Enrage:** below half health a boss roars, gets 20% faster, uses its
  specials more often and the screen says **NAME IS ENRAGED**.
- **Armour:** the katana does only part of its damage until the boss is
  staggered. The shotgun always does full damage.
- **Stagger and DEATHBLOW:** blast a boss to stagger it, then press **F** for
  a **DEATHBLOW** that takes 15% of its max HP. Bosses can't be glory killed
  or plunged.
- **Loud, then quiet:** when a boss falls, a FINISH splash hits, the world
  slows, the music cuts to silence, every enemy left shorts out and explodes,
  and the boss's felled line appears. Then the tally pays a **BOSS BOUNTY**
  of 2000 × the wave.
- **Rewards:** every boss kill unlocks that boss's next weapon look. See
  [Player Level and Rewards](Player-Level-and-Rewards#boss-rewards).

## KUROKAGE, Blade of the Null Signal

A neon-piped cyber-ninja with a long glowing blade and a trailing scarf. He
tests your eyes and footwork rather than your health bar: the lowest HP, the
quickest windups and many small hits.

| Attack | Warning | Damage | How to beat it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dash-slash chain (2 dashes, 3 enraged) | A neon line 16 m long on the floor. It follows you, then locks just before he goes | 20 per dash, with knockback | Step off the line once it locks, then hit him while he recovers |
| Shuriken fan (5 stars, 7 enraged) | A windup sound | 10 each | Slash to deflect them back, or strafe out of the fan |
| Blink | Smoke and a blink sound; he vanishes | none | He reappears 3 m behind you. Turn round on the sound |
| Blink strike | A quick windup right after the blink | 16 | Dash away or blast him first |
| Sword combo (2 hits, 3 enraged) | A 0.45 s windup | 16 per hit | Dash back or sidestep, then punish |

- Sprints in from far away, then circles you, switching direction.
- **Enrage:** a third dash, a third combo hit and 7 shuriken, all faster.
- **Rewards:** KAGEROU EDGE, then KITSUNEBI COIL.

## TETSUGUMO, Chrome Broodmother

A six-legged spider mech with a gatling, missile pods and a glowing core. She
keeps her distance and fills the roof with bullets, missile rings and her
own brood. The biggest body and the slowest mover, but the heaviest armour
against the katana.

| Attack | Warning | Damage | How to beat it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sweeping gatling | A 0.9 s spin-up as the barrel glows | 7 per bolt, 25 bolts over 2 s | Deflect the stream back at her, or run against the sweep |
| Missile strike (7 rings, 9 enraged) | Beeps, glowing pods, then red rings on the floor: the first under you, the rest around it | 20 per missile | Keep moving out of the rings; close in after |
| Call the brood | A roar | none | 2 grunts and a gunner drop in near her. Glory kill them |
| Stomp (when you're within 6 m) | Her body rises for 0.8 s | 22 | **Jump** the shockwave ring |

- Holds 8 to 15 m away: walks in if you're far, backs off if you're close,
  strafes in between.
- **Enrage:** 9 missiles instead of 7, and specials come faster.
- She explodes when she dies.
- **Rewards:** KANAYAGO EDGE, then AKAGANE COIL.

## SHUTEN, the Overclocked Daemon

A chrome oni who walks you down and hits like a truck. The most HP and the
biggest single hits, and always the finale of the first cycle on wave 9.
Named after Shuten-doji, the oni king slain with the blade Dojigiri, the
namesake of your katana.

| Attack | Warning | Damage | How to beat it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Two-hit combo | A 0.7 s windup | 32 per hit, big knockback | Dash back out of reach, then punish |
| Leap slam | A roar as he leaps at you | 26 | **Jump** the shockwave ring (it spreads 24 m) |
| Charge | A roar and a 0.8 s windup | 30, heavy knockback | Dash sideways. He shakes the screen if he hits a wall |
| Fan volley (enraged only) | A windup | 14 per bolt, 9 bolts | Deflect them back, or strafe out |

- Always walks straight at you. No strafing, no retreat.
- **Enrage:** his eyes turn red, he speeds up to 5.6 m/s and the fan volley
  replaces every third leap.
- **Rewards:** ONIBI EDGE, then RAIKO COIL.

## Coming soon

These are built and waiting to go into the game. Until then the bosses play
as described above.

- **KAMAE posture bar.** Aggression fills a bar under the boss's health:
  katana hits fill it most, deflected shots and glory kills on its adds help
  too. It drains if you back off. When it fills, **KAMAE BROKEN**: the boss
  reels, and **F** lands the **ONIKIRI** blow for 20% of its max HP. This
  replaces the DEATHBLOW.
- **Tougher movesets.** Bosses sidestep when you hang back, hold some swings
  a beat longer, gain an area attack each, and get one heavy **RED SIGNAL**
  move with a long warning that you must dash or jump. The first boss on
  wave 3 stays gentle for newcomers.

New bosses and boss variants come later (see the [Roadmap](Roadmap)).
