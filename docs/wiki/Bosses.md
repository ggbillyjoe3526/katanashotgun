# Bosses

**Every third wave is a boss wave** (3, 6, 9 and on). An alarm sounds, the
boss hijacks the screen with test-card colour bars, **BOSS INCOMING** scrolls
across it, and the boss's name card lands with its own music. The boss drops
in **right where you are looking**, usually on your own floor, and lands with
a slam that shakes the screen, so you never have to hunt for it.
Boss waves never have a mutator.

Bosses fight like duelists: they read you, step out of lazy shots, mix quick
strings with held swings, and each has one area attack and one heavy **RED
SIGNAL** move. Every attack is telegraphed, and every big one leaves the boss
open for a moment.

## KAMAE and the ONIKIRI

Bosses can't be staggered, glory killed or plunged. Instead each one has a
gold **KAMAE** bar (its fighting stance) under its health bar, at the top
centre of the screen.

1. **Fill it by attacking.** Katana hits fill it fastest. Shotgun blasts help,
   and so does every shot you deflect back at the boss. A glory kill on one of
   its adds within 15 m fills 12% of the bar in one go.
2. **Don't back off for long.** After 2.5 s without pressure the bar drains,
   7% per second. A quick retreat to heal costs little. The more hurt the
   boss is, the slower it drains: near death only about a third as fast.
3. **Break it.** When the bar fills, a heavy clang, the screen glitches and
   **KAMAE BROKEN** lands on a gold block: the
   boss reels, defenceless, for about 4 to 5 seconds (Kurokage 4.1 s,
   Tetsugumo 4.7 s, Shuten 4.5 s). The bar shows the time left.
4. **Press F for the ONIKIRI.** A red **F ONIKIRI** prompt appears. The blow,
   an X-cut and a point-blank blast, takes **20% of the boss's max HP**, and
   kills it outright if it has 20% or less left. It heals 20 HP and gives 3
   cells, like a glory kill.

After a break the bar starts again from empty, and there's no limit on how
many times you can break a boss.

## Perfect deflects and signature volleys

Every boss has one **signature volley**: shots with a big **white glow**.
Deflect one in the last moment before it hits you, a
[perfect deflect](Weapons#perfect-deflect), and when it flies home it fills
**20% of the boss's KAMAE** on top of its normal hit, with a **PERFECT**
stamp and a moment of slow motion. Each volley pays this once, however many
of its shots you catch.

- **Kurokage:** his whole shuriken fan glows white.
- **Tetsugumo:** each gatling sweep ends with one big white round, slower
  than the stream, aimed straight at you.
- **Shuten:** his fan volley once he's enraged.

This is new and being play-tested: it may change after testing.

## How every boss fights

- **Sidesteps:** hang back and aim at a boss from more than about 7 m and it
  steps out of the line. Get closer, or catch it mid-attack.
- **Edges:** bosses don't throw themselves off ledges. Dashes and charges
  stop at the edge and sidesteps only go where there is floor, so a boss
  never drops into the pit and leaves you waiting.
- **Held swings:** some swings hold a beat longer before they land. The eyes
  stay dim, then flare with a hiss just before the hit. Don't dodge too early.
- **Punish windows:** after every big attack the boss is left open for about
  a second: its eyes dim, its armour drops and your hits fill its KAMAE half
  as fast again. That's your moment.
- **RED SIGNAL:** a spinning red diamond over the boss's head and an alarm
  mean its heavy move is coming, with a beep just before it lands. It can't be
  deflected or traded with: **get clear, or dash through it at the last
  moment** for a **PERFECT**, which slows time briefly and fills 15% of its
  KAMAE.
- **Gentle the first time round:** on waves 3, 6 and 9 the area attack and
  RED SIGNAL wait until the boss is enraged, and the very first boss of a run
  warns you longer. From wave 12 bosses use everything from the start.
- **Enrage:** below half health a boss roars, gets 20% faster, uses its
  specials more often and the screen says **NAME IS ENRAGED**.
- **Armour:** the katana does only part of its damage, except while the boss
  is broken or open after a big attack. Masamune Edge's heavy third cut and the
  shotgun always do full damage.
- **Reinforcements:** 1 or 2 grunts drop in every 7 s (every 10 s against
  Tetsugumo) while fewer than 4 are alive. Glory kill them to fill the boss's
  KAMAE.
- **Seeded:** a boss's choices come from your [seed](Seeds), so the same seed
  gives the same fight.

## Order and scaling

- The first cycle sends Kurokage and Tetsugumo on waves 3 and 6, in an order
  your seed decides, and **Shuten always on wave 9**. After that all three come
  in a seeded shuffle.
- Each time round a boss has more health: about ×1.18 by wave 9 and ×1.72 by
  wave 12. Its damage and KAMAE bar grow half as fast.

## When a boss falls

**Loud, then quiet:** a FINISH splash over the world turned into a
red-and-black negative, the world slows, the music cuts to
silence, every enemy left shorts out and explodes one by one, and the boss's
felled line appears in gold. Then the tally pays a **BOSS BOUNTY** of 2000 ×
the wave, and a reward card shows what you unlocked
([Player Level and Rewards](Player-Level-and-Rewards#boss-rewards)). A boss
killed by the ONIKIRI is tagged **ONIKIRI** and scores double.

## KUROKAGE, Blade of the Null Signal

A neon-piped cyber-ninja with a long glowing blade and a trailing scarf. He
tests your eyes and footwork rather than your health bar: the lowest HP, the
quickest windups and many small hits. The only boss that vanishes and attacks
from behind.

| Attack | Warning | Damage | How to beat it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dash-slash chain (2 dashes, 3 enraged) | A neon line 16 m long on the floor. It follows you, then locks just before he goes | 20 per dash, with knockback | Step off the line once it locks, then hit him while he recovers |
| Shuriken fan (5 stars, 7 enraged) | A windup sound; the stars glow white | 10 each | Slash to deflect them back (fills his KAMAE fast), at the last moment for a PERFECT; or strafe out of the fan |
| Blink | Smoke and a blink sound; he vanishes | none | He reappears 3 m behind you. Turn round on the sound |
| Blink strike | A quick windup right after the blink | 16 | Dash away or blast him first |
| Sword combo (2 hits, 3 enraged) | A 0.45 s windup; some hits are held | 16 per hit | Dash back or sidestep, then punish |
| Smoke ring (area, when you're within 5 m) | Smoke at his feet | 18, a full circle 5 m wide, 0.7 s later | Step out or jump |
| **NULL CROSS** (RED SIGNAL) | Red diamond and alarm; he vanishes and three red lines cross where you stand | 26 per dash, along each line in turn | Get off the lines, or dash through a dash at the last moment |

- Sprints in from far away, then circles you, switching direction. He only
  starts a dash-slash within the line's 16 m, on your level and in sight.
- **Sidestep:** leaves an afterimage in smoke and darts aside.
- **Enrage:** a third dash, a third combo hit and 7 shuriken, all faster.
- **Rewards:** KAGEROU EDGE, then KITSUNEBI COIL.

## TETSUGUMO, Chrome Broodmother

A six-legged spider mech with a gatling, missile pods and a glowing core. She
keeps her distance and fills the roof with bullets, missile rings and her
own brood. The biggest body and the slowest mover, and the heaviest armour
against the katana. The only boss that runs from you and fights through other
enemies.

| Attack | Warning | Damage | How to beat it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sweeping gatling | A 0.9 s spin-up as the barrel glows | 7 per bolt, 25 bolts over 2 s | Deflect the stream back at her (each return fills her KAMAE), or run against the sweep |
| Signature round | Ends every gatling sweep: one big white round | 14 | Deflect it at the last moment for a PERFECT, or step aside |
| Missile strike (7 rings, 9 enraged) | Beeps, glowing pods, then red rings on the floor: the first under you, the rest around it | 20 per missile | Keep moving out of the rings; close in after |
| Call the brood | A roar | none | 2 grunts and a gunner drop in near her. Glory kill them to fill her KAMAE |
| Stomp (when you're within 6 m) | Her body rises for 0.8 s; some stomps are held | 22 | **Jump** the shockwave ring |
| EMP field (area, any range) | Three cyan zones crackle on the floor, one under you | 12 every 0.6 s while you stand in one | Step out, or jump: jumping clears them |
| **BROOD BARRAGE** (RED SIGNAL) | Red diamond, alarm, glowing pods: two closing rings of missile marks around you, with one gap | 20 per missile | Find the gap and run through it, or dash through at the last moment |

- Holds 8 to 15 m away: walks in if you're far, backs off if you're close,
  strafes in between.
- **Sidestep:** leaps back and lands with a small shockwave (12 damage).
- **Enrage:** 9 missiles instead of 7, and specials come faster.
- She explodes when she dies.
- **Rewards:** KANAYAGO EDGE, then AKAGANE COIL.

## SHUTEN, the Overclocked Daemon

A chrome oni who walks you down and hits like a truck. The most HP and the
biggest single hits, and always the finale of the first cycle on wave 9.
Named after Shuten-doji, the oni king slain with the blade Dojigiri, the
namesake of your katana.

| Attack | Warning | Damage | How to beat it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Two-hit combo | A 0.7 s windup; some swings are held | 32 per hit, big knockback | Dash back out of reach, then punish |
| Leap slam | A roar as he leaps at you | 26 | **Jump** the shockwave ring (it spreads 24 m) |
| Charge | A roar and a 0.8 s windup | 30, heavy knockback | Dash sideways. He shakes the screen if he hits a wall |
| Fan volley (enraged only) | A windup; the bolts glow white | 14 per bolt, 9 bolts | Deflect them back, at the last moment for a PERFECT; or strafe out |
| Fissures (area, within 14 m) | His fist hits the floor; five fire lines crack out in a star | 24 | Jump them, or stand between the lines |
| **DAEMON GRAB** (RED SIGNAL, from 9.5 m or closer) | Red diamond and alarm; he aims, then lunges | 34: he grabs you and hurls you | Dash through the lunge at the last moment. A missed grab leaves him open for almost 2 s |

- Always walks straight at you. No strafing, no retreat. He saves his leap,
  charge and fan volley for when he's within 14 m, on your level and in sight.
- **Sidestep:** a quick shoulder barge to the side.
- **Enrage:** his eyes turn red, he speeds up to 5.6 m/s and the fan volley
  replaces every third leap.
- **Rewards:** ONIBI EDGE, then RAIKO COIL.
