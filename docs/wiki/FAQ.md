# FAQ

**Windows says "Windows protected your PC" when I run the installer.**
The game is new and isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. Click **More
info**, then **Run anyway**. See [Getting Started](Getting-Started).

**Does my Player Level make the game easier?**
No. It never changes difficulty or your stats. Boss rewards are cosmetic too.
That keeps scores on the same seed comparable.
([Player Level and Rewards](Player-Level-and-Rewards))

**What's the difference between PLAYER LV and RUN LV?**
RUN LV is the level inside a run that deals [level-up cards](Level-Up-Cards);
it resets every run. PLAYER LV is your lifetime level and never goes down.

**Why can't I glory kill this enemy?**
It has to be **staggered** first (its marker pulses), close (about 4.5 m) and
near your crosshair, with no wall in the way. Shotgun it until it staggers,
then press F. Bosses can't be glory killed: fill and break their KAMAE bar,
then press F for the ONIKIRI. ([Glory Kills](Glory-Kills))

**My katana does almost nothing to the big ones.**
Brutes and bosses are armoured: the katana does only part of its damage. Blast
brutes with the shotgun to stagger them first. Bosses take full katana damage
while their KAMAE is broken and in the moment after a big attack.

**I'm out of cells.**
Use the katana: every katana kill gives a cell back, and so does every
deflect. Glory kills give 3. ([How to Play](How-to-Play#shotgun-cells))

**I clicked a level-up card by accident.**
You can't any more: the first click only selects a card, and a second click
(or the same key, or Enter) takes it.

**Can I play the same run as my friend?**
Yes. Copy your [seed](Seeds) on the pause or results screen and send it;
they click **PLAY A SEED** on the title screen and paste it. The same seed
gives the same map, waves,
mutators, bosses and card offers.

**Does a seed from 0.1 Dev 1 still give the same run?**
No. 0.1 Dev 2 fixed seeds so that how you play a wave never changes the
waves after it, and that changed what every seed gives. To race a friend on
a seed, both play the same version. ([Seeds](Seeds))

**I quit in the middle of a run. Is it lost?**
No. The title screen offers **CONTINUE** from the start of the wave you
were on. ([Saving and Continuing](Saving-and-Continuing))

**Where are my saves, and are they safe if I uninstall?**
On Windows in `%APPDATA%\KatanaShotgun\`. Uninstalling leaves them there.
([Saving and Continuing](Saving-and-Continuing#where-the-files-are))

**I pressed F10 and a DEV SETTINGS screen opened.**
That's the developer's testing menu, not part of the game. Press Esc to
close it. If you switch anything on in its RUN or ACTIONS tabs, a DEV tag
shows in the top right and the run becomes a **DEV RUN**: it never enters
the top 10 and gives no XP or unlocks. **DEFAULTS** in that menu switches
everything back off.

**The game runs slowly.**
In [Settings](Settings), set Graphics to WEAK, then lower the Render scale.
Press F3 in game to see your frame rate.

**Can I use a controller?**
Not yet. It's an idea for later ([Roadmap](Roadmap)).

**Why are the names Japanese?**
Every name in the game comes from Japanese myth or history, given a
cyberpunk twist: Raijin the thunder god arms your shotgun, and Dojigiri, the
blade that slew the oni king Shuten-doji, is your katana. In-game text drops
the long-vowel marks (DOJIGIRI, not Dōjigiri).
