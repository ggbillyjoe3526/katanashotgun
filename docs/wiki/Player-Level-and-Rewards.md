# Player Level and Rewards

## Player Level

Your **PLAYER LV** rises after every run and never goes down. It shows on the
title screen and the results screen.

- **It unlocks nothing and never changes difficulty or stats.** It's simply a
  record of how far you've come.
- Don't confuse it with **RUN LV**, the in-run level that deals
  [level-up cards](Level-Up-Cards) and resets every run.
- When a run ends, the results screen counts your XP into the level bar, and
  every level you gain gets its own fanfare.

### XP from a run

| Row | XP |
| --- | --- |
| WAVES CLEARED | 60 per wave |
| BOSSES FELLED | 250 per boss |
| KILLS | 4 per kill |
| GLORY KILLS | 15 per glory kill |
| SCORE | 1 per 500 points |

Level 2 needs **400 XP**, and each level after needs 150 more than the last
(550 for level 3, 700 for level 4...), until level 31, after which every
level needs the same. A first run that reaches wave 3 is usually enough for
level 2.

The pace is still being tuned, so these numbers may change.

## Boss rewards

Bosses are the only thing that unlock anything, and what they unlock is
**cosmetic only**: new looks for your weapons. No stat boosts, ever.

Each boss has a list of rewards. **Every time you fell a boss, its next
locked reward unlocks**, shown on a reward card after the wave tally. Nothing
is random.

| Boss | 1st kill | 2nd kill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Kurokage](Bosses#kurokage-blade-of-the-null-signal) | **KAGEROU EDGE** (katana): orange heat-haze edge, gold wrap | **KITSUNEBI COIL** (shotgun): green foxfire coils |
| [Tetsugumo](Bosses#tetsugumo-chrome-broodmother) | **KANAYAGO EDGE** (katana): gold edge, orange wrap | **AKAGANE COIL** (shotgun): copper-orange coils |
| [Shuten](Bosses#shuten-the-overclocked-daemon) | **ONIBI EDGE** (katana): red ghost-fire edge, cyan wrap | **RAIKO COIL** (shotgun): magenta coils |

The stock looks are **STOCK EDGE** (cyan) and **STOCK COIL** (blue).

### Changing your look

On the **title** or **pause** screen:

- **1** cycles your **katana edge**.
- **2** cycles your **shotgun coil**.

Your choice is saved with your profile.

More rewards (new maps, palettes, mutators and boss variants) may join these
lists as that content is made.
