# The Map

## Shin-Kagura Rooftop

The neon rooftops of **Shin-Kagura** ("New Kagura", after the sacred Shinto
dance), a cyberpunk Japanese megacity under a striped synthwave sun. It's the
one map for now.

- **The roof:** a 64 × 64 m rooftop with a glowing grid floor and a chrome
  edge lit with neon.
- **The torii shrine:** a raised platform in the middle, 2.4 m high, with
  ramps up.
- **Catwalk decks** on legs along both sides, 3.2 m up. Enemies walk under
  and around them, and they're a good place to [plunge](Glory-Kills#plunge)
  from.
- **4 jump pads** that launch you high into the air.
- **Cover:** 4 to 6 neon-edged crates (one stacked) and a set of obelisks.
  Where they sit changes with the [seed](Seeds).
- **Around you:** buildings with neon roof edges, signs and ads, a sky
  hologram, neon palms, mountains on the horizon and the striped sun, which
  sits somewhere different on each seed.
- Enemies drop in from 18 points around the roof, each marked by a beam of
  light.

## Colour palettes

Each run's seed picks one of five palettes. Every palette mixes six strong
neon colours (pinks, cyans, ambers, electric blues, acid greens and
orange-reds) across the signs, tubes, crates and windows, with a sky that
fades through at least two colours.

- SUNSET DRIVE
- MIAMI NIGHTS
- ULTRAVIOLET
- BLOOD CHROME
- TOXIC TOKYO

## Weather

The seed also picks the weather: **clear** (35% of seeds), **light rain**
(45%) or **heavy rain** (20%). The **RAIJIN'S STORM** mutator brings a
thunderstorm for its wave, with lightning that strikes the roof (see
[Waves and Mutators](Waves-and-Mutators)).
