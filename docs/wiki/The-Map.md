# The Map

## Shin-Kagura Rooftop

The neon rooftops of **Shin-Kagura** ("New Kagura", after the sacred Shinto
dance), a cyberpunk Japanese megacity under a striped synthwave sun. It's the
one map for now, and it's built for going up and down: the middle of the roof
is cut open into a deep shaft, and the north side climbs into a shrine tower.
You start on the south side of the roof.

## Levels

From the bottom up:

| Height | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| −10 m | **The metro platform** at the bottom of the shaft, with a tunnel at each end and the **geyser pad** that throws you back up to the bridge |
| −5 m | **Service ledges** around the shaft: solid blocks north and south, balconies on legs east and west |
| 0 m | **The roof ring** around the shaft: a stairwell hut, crate and container stacks, a tall vent tower, planters and two neon palms |
| +5 m | **The torii bridge** across the shaft, the **catwalks** down both sides, the **north deck** and the **billboard gantry** in the south-east |
| +6 to +8 m | An antenna terrace (east) and a water-tank platform (west) on the north deck |
| +10 m | **The shrine tower** on the north deck |
| +13 m | **The beacon cap** on top of the tower, the highest point on the map |

## The look

The rooftop is built up with detail: raised floor plates with lit seams
between them (some cells are left open), copper pipes, air-con units, vending
machines, lit shop shutters, hanging signs, roof vents, skylights, a small
shrine, and cables strung with paper lanterns across the shaft. Coloured haze
hangs over the city, mist pools in the shaft and drifts below the roof edge,
and lamps throw light and beams onto the floor.

Anything you'd walk into is solid: vending machines, vents, air-con boxes,
crate stacks, the pipe benches along the parapet and street lamps. No prop
on the floor is knee-high: each is either flat enough to run straight over
(the skylights, the drains, the floor plates) or at least a 1.2 m hop high;
only the pipe benches are lower, and they sit right against the parapet.
(The low sides of the roof ramps can still stop you if you run into them.)
None of it stands on a spawn point, a ramp or an enemy's leap spot, and
nothing gives you a step up to a wall that is meant to stay out of reach.
Signs, pipes and air-con units on walls hang above your head.

## Getting around

- **Every level has a ramp.** In the pit, two ramps run along the service
  walls from the metro platform and end on the balconies, so the middle of
  the floor stays open (a low lit wall guards the foot of each ramp's open
  side). Ramps also run from the balconies up to the roof, from the roof to the north
  deck, from the deck to the top of the tower (on its west side) and up to the
  catwalks at their south ends. The bridge joins the two catwalks.
- **Amber edges mark the ledges you can grab.** They're all 5 m tall: jump,
  double jump and [climb](Movement#climbing-ledges). You'll find them around
  the shaft (metro to service ledges, service ledges to the roof), along the
  north deck, the inside of each catwalk, the front of the shrine tower and
  the billboard gantry.
- **3 m blocks** (the stairwell hut, containers, the step up to the gantry,
  the water-tank platform and the beacon cap) take one jump and a climb.
  Low 1.2 m crates are a simple hop.
- **The vent tower** on the east side is 7.5 m of sheer wall. Nothing climbs
  it, you included.
- **Geyser pads** on every level throw you into the air: see
  [Geyser pads](#geyser-pads) below.
- You can drop off any edge. The walls around the roof stop you from falling
  off the map.

## Geyser pads

Nine glowing pads, in the same places on every seed. Walk onto one and it
throws you into the air. Hold a direction to steer while you fly, and your
double jump still works. Hold nothing and you come back down onto the pad,
which throws you up again.

| Where | Height | How far up | Where to land |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Metro platform, south of the bridge | −10 m | about 15 m | the bridge |
| Service ledges, north-west and south-east corners | −5 m | about 9 m | the roof, or the bridge with a double jump |
| South roof, either side of where you start | 0 m | about 9 m | the stairwell hut (west), the billboard gantry (east) |
| Roof beside the shaft's north corners | 0 m | about 9 m | the side catwalks |
| North end of each side catwalk | +5 m | about 9 m | the water-tank platform (west), the antenna terrace (east), the north deck |

Every pad is a good start for a [plunge](Glory-Kills#plunge): look down for
an enemy on the way.

## Plunge spots

Height means [plunges](Glory-Kills#plunge). The best spots:

- **The torii bridge**, over the whole shaft. Enemies on the service ledges
  and the metro platform are right below you.
- **The catwalks**, above the east and west sides of the roof ring.
- **The north deck edge**, over the open strip of roof in front of it.
- **The service ledges**, over the metro platform.
- **The billboard gantry** and the top of the container stack.
- **Any geyser pad's launch** counts too: plunge on the way down, even onto
  an enemy on the floor you launched from.

## Where enemies come from

Enemies arrive from 18 places, each marked by a beam of light:

- **Metro tunnels** at both ends of the metro platform.
- **Freight lifts** on the north and south service ledges, and one beside the
  stairwell hut.
- **Roof hatches** on the west and east sides of the roof and on the north
  deck.
- **Sky drops** onto every level: the open roof, the catwalks, the north deck,
  the gantry and the top of the shrine tower.

Enemies from tunnels, lifts and hatches walk out; the rest fall in from the
sky. They don't stay on their level: they **follow you** up the ramps, drop
off ledges after you, and leap up the amber ledges. Before a leap an enemy
crouches for a moment, so you see it coming. Height buys you a breather, not
a hiding place.

## The city

The towers around the roof have lit windows, neon signs, adverts and the logos
of six corporations: AMATERASU DYNAMICS, TENGU AEROSPACE, INARI FOODS,
TSUKUYOMI MEDIA, OMOIKANE SYSTEMS and KAPPA HYDRO. Flying cars stream past in
four lanes (fewer on [WEAK graphics](Settings#graphics-presets)), a monorail runs
south of the roof, an ad blimp circles and searchlights sweep the sky. The
sun starts somewhere different on each [seed](Seeds) and drifts slowly across
the gap in the skyline to the north. The signs and adverts change with the
seed too. None of it affects play.

## The city is watching

Shin-Kagura notices how well you're doing. This is new and still being tried
out, so it may change.

- **News drones.** TSUKUYOMI MEDIA sends a camera drone for every 4 of your
  [combo](Scoring-and-High-Scores). They hover high above the roof around
  you, above anything you can climb, with their lights pointed your way.
  Up to 4 drones on WEAK graphics, 8 on MEDIOCRE and 12 on INSANE. Their cameras flash when your combo reaches
  10, 25, 50, 75, 100, 150 and 200 (gentler with the flash slider in
  [Settings](Settings)). Once the combo breaks they stay for 5 seconds, then
  fly back into the city one by one.
- **City screens.** Four giant screens high on masts around the roof take
  turns showing two pages. **TSUKUYOMI LIVE** shows your score
  and kills, or your combo while it's 4 or more. The **seed page** shows the
  best initials and score saved on this computer for the run's
  [seed](Seeds), or NO RECORD YET. Only scores in your top 10 are saved, so a
  seed's record is the best run on it that made your top 10.
- **New best.** When your score passes the seed's record, every screen shows
  NEW BEST for a few seconds and the drones' cameras flash. For the rest of
  the run the seed page says you're ahead.

The drones and screens only watch: they never change a fight, and they stay
high above anything you can climb, so nothing hits them.

## Colour palettes

Each run's seed picks one of five palettes. Every palette mixes six strong
neon colours (pinks, cyans, ambers, electric blues, acid greens and
orange-reds) across the signs, tubes, crates and windows, with a sky that
fades through at least two colours.

- SUNSET DRIVE
- MIAMI NIGHTS
- ULTRAVIOLET
- BLOOD CHROME
- TOXIC TOKYO

## Weather

The seed also picks the weather: **clear** (35% of seeds), **light rain**
(45%) or **heavy rain** (20%). Rain leaves **puddles** where water would
collect: in open floor cells and round drains, along the foot of the parapet
and of tall walls, and in low spots. Heavy rain makes more and bigger ones;
a clear night leaves only the metro floor wet (it leaks). Puddles and the
skylights' glass are the only things that reflect the neon (not on WEAK
graphics; see [Graphics presets](Settings#graphics-presets)). The **RAIJIN'S STORM** mutator brings a
thunderstorm for its wave, with lightning that strikes the roof (see
[Waves and Mutators](Waves-and-Mutators)).
