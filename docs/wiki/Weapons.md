# Weapons

You carry two weapons at once, one in each hand, and use both all the time.

## DOJIGIRI katana

**Left hand, left mouse button.** A thermal katana with a glowing edge,
named after the legendary blade that slew the oni king Shuten-doji.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Combo | 3 hits: left to right, a backhand, then an overhead chop |
| Damage | 50 per hit, to **every** enemy in the arc |
| Reach | 3.4 m, in a wide arc in front of you |
| Speed | a swing every 0.32 s (0.45 s after the third hit). The combo resets if you stop for 0.65 s |
| Charges the shotgun | **+1 cell per katana kill** |

- **Armour:** against armoured enemies that aren't staggered, the katana does
  only part of its damage, with sparks and a clang (brutes take 35%, bosses
  35 to 55%). Stagger them first.
- **Staggered enemies** take full damage through armour.

### Deflecting

Slash at an enemy shot as it reaches you and you **deflect** it. It turns
cyan and flies straight back to whoever fired it, locked on, ignoring cover
and other enemies, for **8 times its damage**. A gunner's 12-damage bolt comes
back for 96.

- Every deflect gives **+1 cell** and shows **DEFLECT!**.
- A kill with a returned shot is tagged **RETURN TO SENDER** and scores
  ×1.5.
- If the shooter is already dead, the shot goes to the enemy nearest your
  aim.
- Boss shots can be deflected too: Kurokage's shuriken, Tetsugumo's gatling
  bolts and Shuten's fan volley.

## RAIJIN-12 arc shotgun

**Right hand, right mouse button.** It plays exactly like a shotgun but fires
forking lightning: the "shotgun taser".

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Pellets | 10 per blast, 13 damage each |
| Fall-off | full damage out to 7 m, dropping to 30% by about 22 m. Range 48 m |
| Headshots | **×2 damage**. A blast where 3 or more pellets hit and one hits the head is a headshot, and it pops the head on a kill |
| Fire rate | one blast every 0.72 s |
| Ammo | **cells**: 4 to start, one per blast (see [How to Play](How-to-Play#shotgun-cells)) |
| Stagger | the main way to stagger enemies; every point of damage wears down poise |

All the pellets that hit an enemy add up into one big hit, which knocks it
back and leaves it crackling with electricity for a moment.

## Weapon looks

Bosses unlock new colours for both weapons: glowing katana edges and shotgun
coils. They change only the look. See
[Player Level and Rewards](Player-Level-and-Rewards#boss-rewards).
