# Builds

Every [level-up card](Level-Up-Cards) except Kongo Heart belongs to one or two
**builds**: three cards that play as one style. Own two cards of a build and
it's **LINKED**; own all three and it's **COMPLETE**. There's no hidden set
bonus: the cards themselves are the payoff, and together they change how you
play.

Because some cards sit in two builds, builds overlap. Inazuma Overload joins
STORM RUNNER and THUNDER CHAIN; Ikazuchi Surge joins THUNDER CHAIN and
SHOWSTOPPER; Hyakunin-giri joins EXECUTIONER and SHOWSTOPPER; Idaten Drive
joins STORM RUNNER and GHOST. Once you own one build card, every level-up
offers at least one card that links to it (marked **RECOMMENDED**), so a run
can grow into one or two builds.

| Build | Cards | How it plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [STORM RUNNER](#storm-runner) | Idaten Drive, Fujin's Return, Inazuma Overload | Blast, kill, dash, recharge, blast: never stop moving |
| [THUNDER CHAIN](#thunder-chain) | Raijin's Fork, Ikazuchi Surge, Inazuma Overload | One blast staggers a whole pack |
| [INFERNO](#inferno) | Kagutsuchi Flame, Homura Edge, Hitodama Chain | Fire spreads through the crowd |
| [HEADHUNTER](#headhunter) | Hachiman Focus, Yoichi's Mark, Gashadokuro Burst | Mid-range headshots that pay for themselves |
| [RONIN](#ronin) | Masamune Edge, Susanoo's Wave, Yata Mirror | Count to three; the blade does almost everything |
| [BLOOD ENGINE](#blood-engine) | Chimatsuri Steel, Blood Battery, Sanzu Reprieve | The katana feeds health and cells; live on the edge |
| [EXECUTIONER](#executioner) | Kurama Leap, Hyakunin-giri, Kamaitachi Ring | Glory kill to glory kill across the rooftop |
| [SHOWSTOPPER](#showstopper) | Taiko Frenzy, Hyakunin-giri, Ikazuchi Surge | Stagger everything, glory kill for score |
| [GHOST](#ghost) | Kage Step, Utsusemi Shell, Idaten Drive | Dash through everything, untouchable |

## STORM RUNNER

**Idaten Drive · Fujin's Return · Inazuma Overload**

Every dash charges a cell, every shotgun kill gives the dash back, and your
last cell fires a double blast. The engine runs as long as you keep moving:
dash in, blast, kill, dash again.

- **Tip:** dash *toward* a pack, not away from it. The cell from the dash pays
  for the blast that refunds the dash.
- **Watch out for:** KANASHIBARI (slow dash recharge) and YOMI BLACKOUT (no
  passive recharge) hit this build hardest.

## THUNDER CHAIN

**Raijin's Fork · Ikazuchi Surge · Inazuma Overload**

Arcs leap from every enemy you hit to two more, Ikazuchi makes every hit
stagger harder, and the last-cell blast staggers everything it touches. One
trigger pull can leave a whole crowd reeling for the katana.

- **Tip:** fire into the middle of a group so the arcs have targets to leap
  to. Follow up with slashes while they're staggered.
- **Pairs with:** SHOWSTOPPER (shares Ikazuchi Surge): a staggered crowd is a
  line of glory kills.

## INFERNO

**Kagutsuchi Flame · Homura Edge · Hitodama Chain**

Both weapons set enemies on fire, burning enemies take more katana damage,
and when a burning enemy dies the fire jumps to everyone within 5 m. Crowds
burn themselves down.

- **Tip:** you need Kagutsuchi Flame or Homura Edge before Hitodama Chain can
  be offered. HYAKKI YAKO waves (lots of weak grunts) are an inferno's
  favourite.
- Burning kills are tagged **CREMATED**.

## HEADHUNTER

**Hachiman Focus · Yoichi's Mark · Gashadokuro Burst**

A tight choke with full damage out to 18 m, headshots that refund their cell
and headshot kills that explode. Aim high from mid range and the gun pays for
itself.

- **Tip:** headshots already do double damage; with Yoichi's Mark a steady
  aim means you almost never run dry. Brilliant in SENRYO BOUNTY waves, where
  headshot kills pay double.

## RONIN

**Masamune Edge · Susanoo's Wave · Yata Mirror**

Every third cut is a heavy one that ignores armour and staggers, slashes throw
piercing blood blades, and deflected shots split into three. The blade does
almost everything.

- **Tip:** count your combo. The third hit is the one that breaks armour, so
  save it for brutes. With Masamune Edge, every heavy cut also throws a blood
  blade.
- **Shines against:** TEPPO VOLLEY waves (double gunners): more shots to
  deflect and split.

## BLOOD ENGINE

**Chimatsuri Steel · Blood Battery · Sanzu Reprieve**

Every katana hit heals, healing past full turns into cells, katana kills
charge extra cells, and once a wave a killing blow leaves you on 1 HP. Stay in
the thick of it and the blade keeps you alive.

- **Tip:** a wide slash through a crowd heals once per enemy hit. Fight up
  close.
- **Pairs with:** MURAMASA'S CURSE (you deal and take +50%) if you like living
  dangerously.

## EXECUTIONER

**Kurama Leap · Hyakunin-giri · Kamaitachi Ring**

Glory kill from 9 m away, every glory kill staggers everything within 9 m, and
every one fires a ring of blood blades. One finisher sets up the next, and you
leap from kill to kill across the roof.

- **Tip:** start the chain with a shotgun blast into a group, then press F
  over and over. Each glory kill also heals and gives 3 cells.
- **Pairs with:** SHOWSTOPPER (shares Hyakunin-giri).

## SHOWSTOPPER

**Taiko Frenzy · Hyakunin-giri · Ikazuchi Surge**

Blasts stagger hard, glory kills stagger everything around you, and every
glory kill adds +5 combo and freezes the timer. Built for big combos and big
scores.

- **Tip:** the combo multiplier caps at ×5 at combo 40. Taiko Frenzy gets you
  there fast; keep glory killing to hold it.
- **Great in:** MATSURI RUSH waves, where the frozen timer makes up for the
  faster combo clock.

## GHOST

**Kage Step · Utsusemi Shell · Idaten Drive**

A third dash charge, dashes that slice enemies, and hits that spend a dash
instead of your health. Dash through the crowd, cutting as you go, and nothing
touches you.

- **Tip:** keep one dash charge in reserve so Utsusemi Shell can save you.
  Idaten Drive's extra charge and its cell per dash make that easy.
- Dash kills are tagged **KAGE STEP**.

## Cards outside the builds

**KONGO HEART** (+25 max HP and a full heal, up to 3 times) fits any run and
is a safe pick when nothing else links.
