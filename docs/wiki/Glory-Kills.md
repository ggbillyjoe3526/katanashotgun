# Glory Kills

A glory kill is a short, brutal first-person finisher, and the best thing you
can do to an enemy.

## How to do one

1. **Stagger** an enemy (blast it with the shotgun until its marker pulses).
2. Get close: within about **4.5 m**, roughly in front of your crosshair, with
   no wall in the way.
3. A gold key cap with **F GLORY KILL** appears under your crosshair (below
   your combo) and the enemy's marker turns gold.
4. Press **F**.

You have the whole stagger to do it: 1.8 s on a grunt, 2.2 s on a gunner, 2.8 s
on a brute. Pressing F a split second early still counts. With several
staggered enemies in reach, you get the one nearest your crosshair.

A plain slash on a staggered enemy just cuts it (hard, through its armour).
F is your choice between a quick kill and the full show.

## What you get

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Health | **+20 HP** (40 under the Akatsuki mutator) and 4 extra health orbs |
| Cells | **+3** |
| Points | **×2** on the kill, tagged **GLORY KILL** |
| Safety | you can't be hurt during the finisher, and the world slows to a quarter speed for a second |

Every glory kill gets the full show: three blade cuts across the screen, the
picture sliced and slipping apart for a moment, angled black bars top and
bottom, and **GLORY KILL** (or **SAKAHOKO DROP** for a plunge) landing at the
top of the screen with your points under it. The boss's ONIKIRI gets two red
cuts and a bigger **ONIKIRI!**.

## The finishers

Which one you see depends on where you are:

| Situation | Finisher |
| --- | --- |
| Brute | Climb up, chop the skull, then blast down into the neck |
| You're in the air above the enemy | A falling overhead cut |
| Behind the enemy | Stab through the back and rip upward |
| In front (one of two) | Impale, then the shotgun under the chin |
| In front (the other) | A decapitating slash, then a blast to the chest |

## Plunge

A glory kill from above, always with the katana.

1. Get onto high ground at least **2 m** above an enemy's feet (or get
   launched by a geyser pad: there's one on every level). [The Map](The-Map#plunge-spots) lists the
   best spots.
2. Drop or jump off toward it and look at it.
3. A gold **F PLUNGE** prompt appears. Press **F** to dive down and drive the
   blade through it.

A plunge counts as a glory kill (health, cells and points) with its own kill
tag, **SAKAHOKO DROP**. The higher you start, the further away a target can
be (up to 7 m sideways).

- A plain jump or double jump from the enemy's own floor doesn't count.
- Grunts and gunners can be plunged any time. **Brutes** must be staggered
  first.
- **Bosses can't be plunged.**

## Glory kills and bosses

Bosses can't be staggered, glory killed or plunged. Instead you fill a
boss's **KAMAE** bar by attacking it, and when the bar breaks, **F** lands the
**ONIKIRI**: an X-cut and a point-blank blast for 20% of its max HP. Glory
kills on a boss's adds fill its bar too. See
[Bosses](Bosses#kamae-and-the-onikiri).

## Cards that change glory kills

Four [level-up cards](Level-Up-Cards) are built around glory kills: **Kurama
Leap** (glory kill from 9 m away), **Hyakunin-giri** (staggers everything
around you), **Kamaitachi Ring** (8 blood blades in a ring) and **Taiko
Frenzy** (+5 combo and a frozen combo timer). See the
[EXECUTIONER and SHOWSTOPPER builds](Builds).
