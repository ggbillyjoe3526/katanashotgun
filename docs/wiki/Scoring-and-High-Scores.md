# Scoring and High Scores

Your score comes from two places: **kill points** during the wave, and the
**tally** at the end of it.

## Kill points

Each kill pays **base points × combo multiplier × mutator multiplier**.

| Enemy | Base points |
| --- | --- |
| Grunt | 100 |
| Gunner | 150 |
| Brute | 600 |
| Kurokage | 5000 |
| Tetsugumo | 7000 |
| Shuten | 8000 |

| Kill type | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| Glory kill or plunge | ×2 |
| ONIKIRI (a boss killed by its break blow) | ×2 |
| Headshot kill | ×1.5 |
| Deflect kill (RETURN TO SENDER) | ×1.5 |

## Combo

- Every kill adds **+1** to your combo and restarts a **4 second** timer. Let
  it run out and the combo drops.
- The combo multiplier is **1 + 0.1 per combo**, up to **×5** at combo 40.
- Milestones at 10, 20, 30, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200 and 300 arrive on a blade
  cut: a slash crosses the top of the screen and "X10 COMBO" slams onto a
  leaning plate, which is sliced in half when it leaves. From 20 up the cut
  also slices the picture itself for a moment.
- The Taiko Frenzy card adds +5 combo per glory kill and freezes the timer;
  the MATSURI RUSH mutator halves the timer.

## The end-of-wave tally

When a wave is cleared, the tally adds up **chips** and multiplies them by a
**mult**, Balatro style.

**Chips** (a row shows only if it isn't zero):

| Row | Chips |
| --- | --- |
| WAVE CLEAR | 250 × the wave number |
| KILLS | 25 each |
| GLORY KILLS | 200 each |
| HEADSHOTS | 75 each |
| DEFLECTS | 100 each |
| PERFECT DEFLECTS | 150 each ([perfect deflects](Weapons#perfect-deflect), on top of DEFLECTS) |
| BOSS BOUNTY | 2000 × the wave number (boss waves only) |

**Mult:**

- **1 + 0.1 × the wave number** (×1.4 on wave 4)
- **×1.5 FLAWLESS** if you took no damage all wave
- **× the mutator's score multiplier**, if the wave had one

**Payout = chips × mult.** It slams into your score at the end of the tally.

**Example:** wave 4 with HYAKKI YAKO, 31 kills, 4 glory kills, 6 headshots and
9 deflects, without being hit:

- Chips: 1000 + 775 + 800 + 450 + 900 = **3925**
- Mult: 1.4 × 1.5 × 1.2 = **2.52**
- Payout: **9891**

## High scores

- The **local top 10** keeps each run's initials, score, wave and
  [seed](Seeds).
- Your best score shows at the bottom left of the title screen, and the
  table under **HI-SCORES** on the title. Pass your best mid-run and
  **NEW HI-SCORE** flashes once; the results screen then shows **NEW BEST**.
  (On your very first run there's no score to beat yet, so only NEW BEST
  shows.)
- The results screen shows the table with your run's row lit. If your run
  makes the table, type **3 initials** into that row (letters and numbers,
  Enter to confirm). Your score is saved the moment the run ends, even if you
  quit before typing them.
- High scores live in your profile with the rest of your progress
  ([Saving and Continuing](Saving-and-Continuing)).

Your [Player Level](Player-Level-and-Rewards) and boss rewards never change
your stats, so scores on the same seed are always a fair race.

The game is fully offline, so there are no online leaderboards. To race a
friend, play the same [seed](Seeds) and compare scores. A
[run card](Run-Cards) is a quick way to show them yours.
