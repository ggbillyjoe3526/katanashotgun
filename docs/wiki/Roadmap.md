# Roadmap

What we're working on and what comes after. **These are plans, not
promises:** the order can change as the game is played and tested. What has
already arrived is in the [Patch Notes](Patch-Notes).

## Being built now

- **Boss posture (KAMAE).** A posture bar on every boss that fills as you
  press the attack. Break it and land the **ONIKIRI** blow. Replaces the
  boss DEATHBLOW. ([Bosses](Bosses#coming-soon))
- **Tougher boss fights.** Sidesteps, delayed swings, an area attack each and
  one heavy, clearly warned **RED SIGNAL** move per boss, with clear windows
  to punish them. The first boss stays gentle for new players.
- **The Well.** The rooftop goes vertical: a shaft down to a metro station,
  balconies, a raised north deck, a torii bridge, and a living city around it.
  ([The Map](The-Map#coming-soon-the-well))
- **Easier-to-read text.** Crisper fonts and tidier panels across the HUD,
  menus and level-up cards.

## Next

- **The first public builds.** Versions go 0.1 Dev 1, Dev 2 and on, then
  0.1 Beta 1, Beta 2, and then the full **0.1** release. Each one is posted on
  the [Releases page](https://github.com/ggbillyjoe3526/katanashotgun/releases)
  with its patch notes.
- **Difficulty tuning** past the opening waves, by playtesting. Waves 1 to 3
  stay easy.

## Later

- **New maps:** Tokaido Expressway (a long, open, generated stretch of
  elevated highway and market street), Takamagahara Spire (fight floor by
  floor up a megastructure), Ryugu Arcade (a neon mall) and Kurama Dojo (a
  dojo in the rain).
- **A redesigned thermal katana**, and the final look for the arc shotgun.
- **Boss variants** (the same boss with a new title and new tricks) and
  **new bosses**.
- **More boss rewards:** maps, palettes, mutators and boss variants joining
  the unlock lists. Always cosmetic or new content, never stat boosts.
- **A professional soundtrack and sound design** replacing today's stand-in
  audio.
- Polish for the **Linux and macOS** builds.

## Ideas we haven't decided on

These have come up but aren't planned yet:

- Controller support
- A daily seed and online leaderboards
- A Steam release and cloud saves
