# Roadmap

What we're working on and what comes after. **These are plans, not
promises:** the order can change as the game is played and tested. What has
already arrived is in the [Patch Notes](Patch-Notes).

## Next

- **More public builds.** 0.1 Dev 1 is out and 0.1 Dev 2 is in the works.
  Then come Dev 3 and on, then 0.1 Beta 1, Beta 2, and then the full **0.1**
  release. Each one is posted on the
  [Releases page](https://github.com/ggbillyjoe3526/katanashotgun/releases)
  with its patch notes.
- **Difficulty tuning** past the opening waves, by playtesting. Waves 1 to 3
  stay easy.

## Later

- **New maps:** Tokaido Expressway (a long, open, generated stretch of
  elevated highway and market street), Takamagahara Spire (fight floor by
  floor up a megastructure), Ryugu Arcade (a neon mall) and Kurama Dojo (a
  dojo in the rain).
  We're also thinking about runs that **travel from map to map**: beat a
  map's boss and the fight carries on somewhere new, with the seed choosing
  the order. Not decided yet.
- **The final look for the arc shotgun**, like the new DOJIGIRI katana
  model. The katana's stock edge colour may still change.
- **Boss variants** (the same boss with a new title and new tricks) and
  **new bosses**.
- **More boss rewards:** maps, palettes, mutators and boss variants joining
  the unlock lists. Always cosmetic or new content, never stat boosts.
- **A professional soundtrack and sound design** replacing today's stand-in
  audio.
- Polish for the **Linux and macOS** builds.

## Ideas we haven't decided on

These have come up but aren't planned yet:

- Controller support
- A daily seed (made from the date, so it works offline)
- A Steam release and cloud saves

## Ideas to try

These will be built to play-test first, and only stay in the game if they
feel right:

- **Seed duels:** share a code for your run, and a friend playing the same
  seed sees whether they're ahead of your score, wave by wave.
- **Two hands, one rhythm:** extra combo for switching between katana and
  shotgun kills.
- **Perfect deflect:** now in the game to play-test (see
  [Weapons](Weapons#perfect-deflect)). It stays if it feels right.
- **Bet on the wave:** choose whether to take each wave's mutator for its
  score bonus.
- **On the beat:** big moments flash and slam in time with the music.
- **Challenge codes:** share a dare, not just a seed: a seed with rules you
  picked, like a mutator on every wave and a score to beat.
- **Ghost runs:** send a friend your best run as a small file, and it plays
  beside them as a neon ghost while they play the same seed.
- **Legendary seeds:** hand-picked seeds on the title screen with bronze,
  silver and gold scores to beat.
- **The city is watching:** now in the game to try: see
  [The Map](The-Map#the-city-is-watching).
- **Run cards:** one key saves a picture of your run with its seed code,
  ready to post anywhere. In the game to try now: see
  [Run Cards](Run-Cards).
- **Soda cans:** INARI FOODS drinks to grab around the roof. Press E and
  you holster the shotgun, cut the top off the can with the katana and down
  it in one go, for a short, strong boost (more speed, healing cuts, double
  score) followed by a milder sugar crash. Coming soon: the developer is
  play-testing it first, so it isn't switched on in the game yet.

KATANA / SHOTGUN is and stays fully **offline**: no accounts, no servers and
no online leaderboards. You race friends by sharing seeds instead.
