# Level-Up Cards

Every time your **RUN LV** goes up, the game pauses and deals **three cards**.
Pick one. Cards don't add a few percent here and there: each one changes how
a part of the loop works, and cards that share a [build](Builds) play as one
style.

## How picking works

- **Click a card (or press 1, 2 or 3) to select it.** It lifts, its outline
  turns white and a box under the cards asks **TAKE ...?**. **Click it again,
  press the same key again, or press Enter** to take it. Clicks from the fight in the first instant after the cards appear
  are ignored, so you can't grab one by accident.
- Some cards have **ranks** (shown as "max 2" or "max 3" below). Picking one
  again ranks it up.
- Several level-ups in a row deal one hand after another.
- A card that links to one you already own wears a gold **RECOMMENDED** tab.
  Once you own a build card, every hand holds at least one card that links to
  it (while one is left).
- The bottom of each card names its builds and the count you'd have after
  picking it, such as **RONIN 2/3**. Picking the second card of a build says
  **LINKED**, the third **COMPLETE**. The pause screen lists your builds in
  its top right corner.
- The hands you're dealt come from your [seed](Seeds): the same seed and the
  same picks give the same cards.

Each card has a ribbon along the top in its colour, with the card's tag and
its key (1, 2 or 3), then its name, its rank, what it does in full, and its
builds along the bottom. Card colours: KATANA cyan, SHOTGUN blue, GLORY
pink, DASH green, BODY gold.

## Glory kill cards

These work on regular enemies; bosses don't get glory kills. A glory kill on
a boss's add still fills the boss's [KAMAE](Bosses#kamae-and-the-onikiri).

| Card | Max | What it does | Builds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **KURAMA LEAP** | 1 | Glory kill from 9 m away instead of 4.5 m: you leap to the target | EXECUTIONER |
| **HYAKUNIN-GIRI** | 1 | Every glory kill staggers every enemy within 9 m (not bosses), ready for the next one | EXECUTIONER, SHOWSTOPPER |
| **KAMAITACHI RING** | 1 | Every glory kill fires 8 blood blades out in a ring around you (60 damage each) | EXECUTIONER |
| **TAIKO FRENZY** | 1 | Every glory kill adds +5 to your combo and freezes the combo timer for 3 s | SHOWSTOPPER |

## Body cards

| Card | Max | What it does | Builds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **KONGO HEART** | 3 | +25 max HP and a full heal | none |
| **SANZU REPRIEVE** | 1 | Once per wave, a killing blow leaves you on 1 HP instead, with 1.5 s of safety | BLOOD ENGINE |

Card names come from Japanese myth and history: Raijin and Fujin are the
thunder and wind gods, Masamune the legendary swordsmith, Susanoo the storm
god, Yata no Kagami the sacred mirror, and Sanzu the river between life and
death.
