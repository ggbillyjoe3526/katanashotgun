# Movement

You're fast. Stand still and the horde catches you.

| Move | Key | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run | W A S D | 8.8 m/s, quick to start and stop |
| Jump | Space | your feet rise about 1.75 m |
| Double jump | Space in the air | about 4 m at its top, with a small push toward the keys you're holding (none held: straight up). It comes back when you land, use a jump pad or climb |
| Dash | Shift | a 0.2 s burst at 21 m/s. **You can't be hurt** for 0.3 s |
| Ledge climb | jump at a ledge and hold forward | grab and pull yourself up |
| Jump pads | walk onto one | launches you high into the air |

## Dashing

You have **2 dash charges**. Each comes back 1.4 s after you use it. Dash
through attacks, not just away from them: you can't be hit during a dash.

Cards change dashing a lot: **Idaten Drive** adds a third charge and makes
every dash charge a cell, **Kage Step** slices enemies you dash through, and
**Utsusemi Shell** spends a dash instead of your health when you get hit. See
the [STORM RUNNER and GHOST builds](Builds).

## Climbing ledges

Jump at a ledge that's just out of reach and keep pushing toward it while
looking at it. Your hands grab the edge and you pull yourself up, keeping some
speed forward. Slashes and dashes pressed during the climb fire as it ends.

- Only ledges up to about **2 m above your feet** can be grabbed. From flat
  ground that means about 3.75 m with one jump and about 6 m with the double
  jump. Anything taller can't be climbed.

## Using height

- Higher ground means [plunges](Glory-Kills#plunge): drop onto a foe and
  press F.
- Jump over shockwaves: Tetsugumo's stomp and Shuten's leap slam send rings
  along the floor.
- Enemies path under and around the raised decks, so height buys you a
  moment.
