### Graphics presets: WEAK, MEDIOCRE and INSANE
- The Quality setting is now **Graphics**, with three presets that change how the game looks, not just how many effects it shows.
- **WEAK** is the game's look so far, made for older PCs.
- **MEDIOCRE** (the new default) adds ambient occlusion, shadows from the moon and the brightest lamps, wider neon glow, a soft haze around every lamp and smoother edges.
- **INSANE** goes all out: light that bounces off the neon onto the floors, wet floors that reflect the city, real fog lit by the lamps, shadows from every lamp and even more blood per kill. It needs a strong graphics card and a game restart (Settings offers a RESTART GAME button).
- If you were on HIGH, you're now on MEDIOCRE, which looks the same and better.
