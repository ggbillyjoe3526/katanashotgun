### New shotgun model (preview)
- A detailed new model of the RAIJIN-12 arc shotgun, made in Blender: an
  angular receiver, two flat barrels with a square emitter, slanted cooling
  vents and a square drum of power cells that turns a quarter turn after
  every blast, in red, gunmetal and off-white.
- For now it's only shown when the DEV menu's SHOTGUN MODEL is set to NEW, so
  it can be checked in both the retro and the plain look before it replaces
  the old shotgun.
