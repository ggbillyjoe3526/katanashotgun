### Run cards
- New: press P on the results screen (or click SAVE RUN CARD) to save a picture of your run: score, wave, bosses felled, builds, your best moment and the seed, in your run's neon colours.
- Cards go in your Pictures folder, in KatanaShotgun. Press O (or click OPEN FOLDER) to open it with the card picked out, ready to drop into Discord or a text.
