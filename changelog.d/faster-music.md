### Faster, glitchier music
- The stand-in soundtrack is rewritten as fast, futuristic synthwave:
  combat tracks run at 140 to 152 beats per minute with driving drums,
  rolling 16th-note basses and bright arpeggios, boss themes go faster
  still, and the title theme has a beat.
- It is dirty on purpose: growling distorted basses and leads, gritty drums,
  and phrase endings that stutter, tape-stop, reverse and bitcrush.
- Real, professionally made music will replace these stand-ins later.
