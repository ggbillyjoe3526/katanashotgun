### Developer menu for testing
- A hidden DEV menu on F10 for testing and play tests. It can switch off the
  PS1 pixel look for a plain, full-resolution picture, and has god mode, an
  autoplay bot, game speed, start wave, forced bosses and mutators, and quick
  actions like skipping a wave.
- Runs that use its cheats are marked DEV RUN and never reach the top 10 or
  give XP or unlocks.
